5. Latest NS-2 work: findings, failures and validation caveats

Read locator findings → matched-history findings → candidate rejection. The locator is adaptive exploration. Its subsequent packet predictions were frozen before recording. At Threshold 3:00, probes −56/−54/−44 dB are each presented after 24 s digital silence and after 2 s at −12 dB, with 8 s observation windows, at both Decay extremes.

  • Zero xruns, no digital rail hits; closing bypass agrees with locator within .00157 dB. A residual one-sample loopback offset in the minimum-Decay take cannot explain second-scale gain changes. Output silence does not prove complete hidden-state reset.
  • Quiet-start curves agree across Decay extremes within .037/.022 dB RMS over .1–1.5 s at −56/−54 dB. At −54 dB, their later trajectories differ substantially; opening is not globally Decay-independent.
  • At −54 dB/minimum Decay, quiet/loud histories converge to a .042 dB difference late in the window, contradicting the original fixed 7.56 dB latch gap. Stronger inputs still open in the first valid 1 ms analysis bin.
  • A threshold shift cannot create the missing positive slope. A universal one-second attack fails: the early −56 dB exponential extrapolates with 2.02 dB late RMS error and would not preserve prompt strong-input opening.
  • Five fitted alternatives: asymmetric envelope; simultaneously leaky envelope; nonlinear bounded charge; continuous switch reusing existing states; the latter with a capped opening time.
  • Packet-only fits can reduce near-threshold RMS 5.177 → about .446 dB, but older note tails regress. Forty-eight older captures were checked, expanding the original 39-take set with nine earlier plucks.
  • Joint fits use original steps/jumps and recalibrate a monotone Threshold map. Complete new input levels are excluded from fitting residuals; 31 older plucks/phrases/soft/slow-stair takes are also excluded. Capped −56-excluded quiet-start error remains 8.36 dB RMS at 48 kHz. Older validation median rises from 1.487 to 2.084–2.167 dB.
  • Not an independent blind holdout: family selection has seen the data, and one optimization start uses the all-history optimum. Exclusion refers to residuals. This is explicitly documented; failed fits do not prove every continuous architecture is impossible.
  • The uncapped rise grows effectively infinite at very weak input and stops following slow steps. A cap fixes part of this defect but not overall generalization. Fitted 30–63 s caps and changed Threshold maps are rejected optimization parameters, not pedal constants.
  • No listening verdict, recovered circuit, identified extra state, or accepted new production coefficients. Keep the existing DSP. Provisional future gates: <.5 dB trajectory RMS, <.25 dB late bias, history-gap error <.5 dB, prompt opening preserved, no material note-tail regression. These are engineering criteria, not audibility thresholds.

The user was shown measured early/late opening, packet fit versus level-exclusion refits, and older note/staircase regressions. Do not confuse model curves beyond the plotted trusted measurement bins with validated behavior.
